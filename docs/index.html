What it does

Sharia screening built in

Only KMI-30 companies, pulled live from PSX. Each alert carries that company's non-compliant income ratio from Al-Meezan, so you know exactly what to purify.

Respects settlement

No sell alert before your shares actually settle into your account, so you are never told to sell something you do not yet possess.

Exit planned at entry

Stop-loss and take-profit are set from the stock's own daily range (ATR) the moment the alert fires, and the position is closed out after 15 days either way.

Runs without you

Scheduled in the cloud every trading day at 17:45 PKT. Nothing to install, no terminal, no laptop that has to stay on.
